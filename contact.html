Quick answers

How do I get a refund on a ticket?

Refunds for online bookings can be requested up to 2 hours before showtime from your booking confirmation email, or through Manage Booking.

I didn't get my booking confirmation.

Check your spam folder first. If it's still missing after 15 minutes, contact us with your payment reference and we'll resend it.

Do you offer group or corporate bookings?

Yes — select "Partnership / business enquiry" above and include your group size, preferred cinema, and date.

Know before you go

Ratings
Guide

We show every film's rating exactly as it was certified in its country of origin. Here's what each system's ratings actually mean, so you can pick the right movie for the right audience.

We show the original rating

Instead of translating every film into one local scale, we display the certification each film actually received in its country of origin — Hollywood titles carry their MPA rating, UK films their BBFC rating, and Pakistani productions their local U/A certification. You'll see it right alongside genre and runtime on every listing.

United States · MPA

Hollywood ratings

Assigned by the Motion Picture Association's Classification and Rating Administration (CARA).

G

General Audiences

All ages admitted

Nothing that would offend parents for viewing by children — minimal violence, no drug content, no strong language.

PG

Parental Guidance

Some material may not suit young children

Parents are urged to give "parental guidance" — may contain brief violence or mild language.

PG-13

Parents Strongly Cautioned

Some material inappropriate for under 13

May go beyond PG in theme, violence, language, or suggestive content, without crossing into R territory.

R

Restricted

Under 17 requires accompanying adult

Contains adult material — intense violence, strong language, sexual content, or drug use.

United Kingdom · BBFC

British ratings

Assigned by the British Board of Film Classification.

U

Universal

Suitable for ages 4+

Content is unlikely to upset children, though it's impossible to predict every reaction at this age range.

PG

Parental Guidance

General viewing, some scenes unsuitable for young children

Parents should consider whether content may upset younger or more sensitive children.

15

Fifteen

No one under 15 admitted

Content not suitable for children under 15 — may include strong language, violence, or discriminatory themes.

18

Eighteen

Adults only

No age limit on content — suitable only for adults.

Pakistan · CBFC / PFCB / SBFC

Local & Urdu/Punjabi films

Pakistani-produced films are certified locally by the relevant censor board — the Central Board of Film Censors for Islamabad, Rawalpindi, KP, and Balochistan; the Punjab Film Censor Board for Punjab; and the Sindh Board of Film Censors for Sindh.

U

Universal

Unrestricted public screening

Suitable for all audiences — minimal violence, no sexual content, no abusive language.

A

Adult

Restricted / parental guidance required

May include stronger violence, intense themes, or language not suitable for young or sensitive viewers.

Common questions

Quick answers

Why don't you just use one rating system for everything?

Converting every film into a single local scale loses nuance — an R-rated US film and an 18-rated UK film aren't always comparable content-wise. Showing the original rating gives you the most accurate picture of what the film's own raters flagged.

Can children watch an 'R' or 'A' rated film?

Restricted ratings (R, 18, A) mean the content is intended for mature audiences or requires parental guidance. Cinema staff may use discretion for younger viewers accompanied by a parent, but the content itself isn't intended for unsupervised children.

What if a film's home country doesn't use one of these systems?

For films from countries without a listed system here, we display the closest available regional or distributor-assigned rating, noted on the film's listing page.
